## Thesis

Three guests read the week's market shock (Nvidia/Jensen Huang hit, repo and liquidity stress, a stronger dollar) as the start of a political and financial discontinuity rather than an ordinary correction. Collum frames it as a bubble that was "looking for a pin". Kunstler lays out a scenario in which the November 2026 midterm was fraudulent and Trump declares an emergency, voids it and orders a rerun before the new Congress is seated. Luongo will not go that far but agrees on the shape: entrenched European/UK/NGO "globalist" networks are fighting Trump, Xi and Putin, and the next weeks or months will be disorderly. The back half drifts into Iran, Europe, the Democratic and GOP establishments, pharma, and the media personalities Fuentes, Carlson and Owens. It ends abruptly because two guests had another show.

## Main arguments

### Markets and liquidity
- **Collum:** the AI bubble "has popped" and the toothpaste won't go back in the tube, though there may be pauses. He cites a book on the 1920s Florida land bubble (title as transcribed: "Bubble under the Sun"). In it a ship capsized in Miami Harbor and took 38 days to clear, and by then the bubble had burst. Hurricane Charlie and Bernanke's rate hikes are his 2008 analogy (Charlie was one trigger, not the cause).
- **Collum:** distinguish the proximate trigger (irrelevant) from the real risk, which is super-valuations, excessive leverage and debt. Markets were "looking for a pin". He compares this to the George Floyd riots.
- **Collum:** he moderated a ZeroHedge debate the night before. Jim Bianco and "Brett" [Brent] Johnson treated the market as generic and not in trouble, and he says they eventually conceded. He recalls Bianco describing a bubble on a panel a year earlier ("revolutionary new tech, way overpriced") and not calling it one.
- **Luongo:** valuation compression is happening alongside a stronger dollar. The dollar was "almost uncontrollably stronger" last week. The "Eurodollar index" (his name for USDX) closed Q3 above 102, which he calls a subtle but real signal.
- **Luongo:** between Wednesday and Friday he saw a 15 bp correction in the front end of the (transcribed) "social/silver futures curve" and most of the yield curve. He reads this as possibly past peak dollar, with repo liquidity returning. Monday was a "liquidity opening moment". Another guest suggested it was a dead-cat bounce.
- **Luongo's parallel:** repo-market lockup in Sept 2007, then Bear Stearns about six months later. Repo lockup in Sept 2019, then COVID. He says there is a liquidity shortage now. Tommy had not seen news of a repo lockup.
- **Luongo:** the Nvidia/Jensen attack is "the big story". Nvidia is the nexus for the liquidity holding valuations up, and the fight is open-source vs closed-source vs government-controlled AI. He says Europe/Brussels/London want control. He puts the attack a week after the election.
- **Luongo:** about $200B was drained from credit markets by the SpaceX and Anthropic IPOs. Anthropic reportedly "just got $110B". He claims Anthropic is a conduit to "recapitalize USAID" and a public-benefit-company fund is how it gets around rules. Another speaker asked how that mechanism works and objected that a private company can't run foreign policy. Luongo says Hegseth booted Anthropic early in the year.
- **Luongo, capital flows:** US asset inflows, not outflows. EUR "at a dollar nine and falling" [garbled], oil heading back to the 70s or low 80s, US-Europe bond spreads widening back toward the US after a counter-trend rally, Fed and BOJ meetings about two weeks out. Gold, silver and Bitcoin are drifting down (collateral being "attacked"). Citing Brent Johnson, he says everyone is still short dollars. He sees possible near-term financial "deflation" and asks who can handle a higher cost of capital. Powell's hikes to 5.5% forced others into riskier moves, including Lagarde's alleged "40-year yield curve control".
- **Kunstler:** what happens to the currency, stock, bond and debt "three-legged stool" if there is a political discontinuity? It will be chaotic.

### Kunstler's election and emergency scenario
- The midterm was "entirely fraudulent". Trump is "playing possum", pretending to cooperate with Democrats.
- He cites an executive order (said as "1 3 4 8 4 8 or something"; renewed by Biden four times and by Trump in September). It lets the president act on foreign influence in elections and requires a DNI report within 45 days of the election. Kunstler thinks Trump is waiting for that report.
- Evidence he cites: turnout stats (Republican show rate about the same as four years earlier within 0.25%, Democrats "40% higher", about 20M). A speaker asked where the stats came from, and it was not answered. He also cites the X account "Data Republican" (named as "Jenica Pound", spelling uncertain). He says it unearthed hundreds of hours of NGO Zoom calls on a planned "color revolution". Luongo says she is a former DoD contractor and a conduit for the administration. He adds that the "two colonels and a general" cite her.
- The NGO plan as Kunstler describes it: provoke Trump into an emergency or insurrection order, call him a tyrant, hand executive governance to a panel of blue-state governors (Newsom, Pritzker, Shapiro, Healey, the Arizona governor), then hold a constitutional convention.
- He claims Jeffries, Schumer and new Democratic members have made "garish" threats of lawfare against Trump, his sons and grandchildren, administration staff and lawyers.
- Trump's response, in Kunstler's telling: declare an emergency, nullify the election, and offer a new election within 90 days with paper ballots, one-day voting and proof of citizenship. Meanwhile expect grid-down episodes and possible sabotage or illegal-alien "street warfare".
- This must happen before the new Congress is sworn in or after certification, and he says "before Christmas, probably".
- Trump supposedly told Europeans, and carried a message to Xi, that the next 90 days in the US will be "spicy" and not to worry or try anything. He also suggests the US told Russia and China it was at DEF CON 1 and that this wasn't about them.
- Collum asked him to define "spicy". Kunstler admits he has no verification, "I have an idea but I can't verify it".
- He expects the color-revolution evidence to open prosecutions, starting with current actors and going back to Jack Smith, Merrick Garland and Christopher Wray. He says Trump has been waiting for a "magic moment" to act. The group notes the report that Brennan's son was arrested over child porn. One guest calls such charges a standard ploy that can be planted, and another asks whether it could be "our guys" fighting back.
- On the Democrats: with USAID dismantled, the party is a "racketeering operation" for NGO jobs and elite overproduction. Billionaire funders (Soros, "Weis"/Wyss in Switzerland, Singham in Shanghai, the Arabella network) are rehiring the displaced to run a color revolution.
- On the GOP: Thune won't confirm executive nominees (including Navy secretary) to damage Trump. Kunstler says "there's got to be and probably will be a new Tea Party" to run them out.

### Luongo's reading of the political fight
- He does not buy the "fraud" base case but will entertain it. His view is a mix of that and the Democrats' own narrative, with the same outcome either way: after the result, the Nvidia/Jensen attack follows, aimed at open-source AI and at building a regulated "1984 on steroids" under government control. He says the result moved the Senate from an expected 53 to 56 and the House from 230 to 260 [numbers and party likely garbled].
- Spooks vs soldiers: Hegseth, Besant and Rubio are the loyalists being targeted. Trump got rid of Susie Wiles and "everybody" is trying to use her to wedge Trump from Rubio. Trump still controls the military.
- He is down on Massie ("fake sovereigntist") for trying to push articles of impeachment against Hegseth, and on the 11 Democrats who refused to advance them.
- Democrats have an emergent civil war (DSA vs Schumer-style establishment, with Fetterman-type moderates wanting normalcy). The GOP has already had one (MAGA vs Thune).
- Model: five- or six-player games collapse into two-player games ("Dune" turning into chess). When Trump showed up, UK, France and Germany stopped fighting each other and united against him.
- Voters: Americans have disliked their leadership for 10 years and swing back and forth. Luongo says Steve Murray pointed out that 15 years of continuing resolutions meant systems could be patched and not fixed. With a budget now, multi-year contracts can start, but the fixes take years. Meanwhile 20-year-olds in a bad labor and cost environment are susceptible to any narrative.
- He says ordinary workers need to be walked into this slowly and may break toward the left or toward vigilantism.

### Middle East, Iran, Europe
- A guest raised Jim Rickards' report that the Saudi family may be "going out of business" and asked how the Houthis are beating Saudi Arabia while Iran is blockaded and its banking routes closed (by Besant). One guest (likely Luongo) concludes support must come from London and the EU, and says the Israelis aren't backing the Houthis against the Saudis. The same guest says someone is attempting a coup in Poland and launching drones into Russia through NATO airspace. Another guest compares it to ISIS driving Hiluxes through Syria and the Mujahideen vs the Soviets.
- Collum says an intelligence-adjacent contact he won't name said he was "shocked" the US hadn't used tactical nukes on Iran. Luongo says he discounts that "to zero" and calls confusing information the hallmark of an intelligence operation, and says commentators are targets for it. Collum says the remark didn't match what he'd been told about the Iran scenario.
- Luongo: Trump's aim in Iran is to separate the civilian government from the IRGC and its "microocracy". He maps parts of the IRGC network to France and bankers to London's Square Mile. If Trump wanted to glass Iran he could have. The difficulty is execution. A larger play may be de-escalation and clearing out the assets built up for "World War III". Trump is pulling parties together under a "Board of Peace" and a Gaza rebuild.
- Luongo: the three leaders (Trump, Xi, Putin) met in "Shenzhen" [garbled] and agreed on four points, all saying the post-WWII architecture, MAD doctrine and energy starvation don't serve people.
- "The turd in the punch bowl" is Europe and the UK. Luongo calls the root problem old European colonialism (CFA franc, Sahel extraction, Square Mile, Hong Kong). One guest says Europe has "no mojo"; Luongo says it will play every card, including false flags to start WWIII.
- Luongo on Lagarde: the video of her with band-aids and a hasty outfit looked like either a warning or an assault. He floats that she is leaving the ECB to enter French politics in 2027, and jokes about her being "thrown down the stairs". A guest adds a "black eye squad" gag (Bernie Sanders, John Roberts).
