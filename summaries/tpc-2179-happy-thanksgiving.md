## Main arguments

### Historical / intelligence (mostly Watkins)
- **Operation Sunrise to Gladio.** Dulles (OSS) and Gen. Lemnitzer met German Gen. Wolff at Operation Sunrise. This produced the hand-over to the US of Reinhard Gehlen's stay-behind networks in German-occupied territory. Funding ran covertly through the Marshall Plan and Rockefeller foundations.
- **World Commerce Corporation (WCC).** Grew out of the British American Canadian Corporation, set up by Bill Donovan. Its original job was selling wartime surplus. She says the Marshall Plan funded it to retrofit and swap the caches' German weapons for standardized NATO/US material. "Detachment A" (declassified 2014, "predecessor of special forces") did the swaps; there is video on its website.
- **Why swap the weapons.** She says discovered caches could not be traced to Nazis, because the same networks were later used for domestic terror. Standardized weapons also gave plausible deniability. She notes taxpayers paid for the surplus twice.
- **JFK and de Gaulle.** She says the French OAS (Gladio element) had 3-6 trained assassins in Dallas on 22 Nov 1963. The same people were tied to 30+ attempts on de Gaulle. Lemnitzer was NATO commander when de Gaulle removed NATO from France. The host adds that Lemnitzer authorized Operation Northwoods (false flag for Cuba).
- **OSS "false history".** The OSS was staffed by direct-commissioned Wall Street lawyers, bankers and PR people (Camp X, Camp Ritchie, Kissinger among the German-Jewish recruits). Its wartime mission was "protecting the investments" of the Wall Street / City of London oligarchs, not helping the Allies. Roughly half of OSS officers went into the CIA; a large contingent went into the WCC. On the recent plan to re-create the OSS (discussed in a prior episode, not explained here), she says the positive is the glorification of the OSS, but it is "false history", and the move looks like a "reset" to WWII-era entities. She would not have made that decision.
- **Strategic bombing.** Both guests say target lists followed financial interests. IG Farben's HQ survived and became Eisenhower's command center. Railways to the camps were not bombed because IG Farben and IBM (Hollerith machines) used the camps as work plants. The camps kept their resources because they were "work plants". Murray says he "can't unsee" how the system works from the inside.
- **Smedley Butler / "War Is a Racket".** The host cites Butler, and Murray says "his book is very timely".
